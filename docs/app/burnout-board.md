# Burnout Board Help

{: .note }
This page has not been checked against the current app yet. The pages listed under [Page by page]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/app/) as checked are up to date.


## What It Does

Monitor staff wellbeing by tracking overtime, consecutive days worked, and leave patterns.

## Why It Matters

Catch potential burnout before it happens. Happy, rested staff provide better service and stay longer.

## How To Use It

1. Review the **burnout risk indicators**
2. Click on **flagged employees** to see details
3. Take action to **reduce risk** (encourage leave, reduce hours)

## Tips

- Check the board weekly
- Have conversations with high-risk employees
- Encourage staff to use their leave
