# Rule Book

**Who uses it:** admins. Address: `/admin/rule-book`.

## What it does

Your rule book lists every leave and pay rule Timebreez follows for your organisation. It is written for your organisation and published by Timebreez. You read each rule and answer it.

## How to use it

1. The first time, press **Open my rule book**. If you see "No rule book has been prepared for this organisation yet", yours is not published yet.
2. Read each rule. For each one, answer one of:
   - **I confirm this**
   - **This is not right**
   - **I have a question**
3. Add your words. They are kept with your name and the date.
4. Changing your mind adds a new answer beside the old one. Nothing is overwritten or hidden.

## Part B: the legal minimum

Part B is Ireland's statutory minimum as Timebreez holds it. It has not been reviewed by a competent reviewer and is not legal advice. You are not asked to confirm it, so Part B has no answer buttons. That review is a separate step, done by someone outside your organisation.

## A newer edition

When Timebreez publishes a newer edition, a notice appears. Press **Open the newer rule book** to read and answer it. The edition and its fingerprint are shown at the top, so you always know which version you answered.
