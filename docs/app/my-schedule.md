# My Schedule Help

{: .note }
This page has not been checked against the current app yet. The pages listed under [Page by page]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/app/) as checked are up to date.


## What It Does

See your personal work schedule - when you're working, in which room, and your upcoming shifts.

## Why It Matters

Know exactly when and where you need to be. No more checking the noticeboard or calling the office.

## How To Use It

1. View your **upcoming shifts** at the top
2. Scroll down to see the **full week view**
3. Tap on any **shift to see details**
4. Check the **room assignment** and times

## Common Questions

### Why don't I see my shifts?

The page shows "No shifts scheduled" when you have no shifts on the roster for that period. Ask your manager to check the roster. Before 17 November 2026 this message also showed for staff who did have shifts. That is fixed.

### Can I request a shift change?

Contact your manager directly to discuss shift swaps or changes. They can update the roster for you.

## Tips

- Check your schedule regularly - it may update
- Note the room assignment for each shift
