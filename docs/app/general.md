# Timebreez Help

## What It Does

Timebreez is **frontline workforce management**. It answers one operational question:

> Do we have the right people, in the right places, at the right times, and does the system accurately reflect what actually happened?

It is built for frontline SMBs, where staffing, location, time, coverage and compliance matter: creches and nurseries, cafes and hospitality, clinics and care, retail, and similar operations. Childcare is our first vertical, not the limit of the product.

## Why It Matters

Frontline operations run on three stages, and Timebreez keeps all three in one place.

| Stage | What it covers |
|-------|----------------|
| **Plan** | Who should be working, where, when, doing what, and what coverage is required |
| **Reality** | Who actually turned up; absences, lateness, clocking, shift changes and exceptions |
| **Pay** | Turning what really happened into payable hours, leave, payroll outputs and an auditable record |

Compliance runs across all three. The goal is not merely that staff are *scheduled*, but that you can show the operation had the right people, coverage and qualifications to run correctly.

Timebreez is **not** primarily a leave tool, a rota or calendar app, an HR system, or payroll software. It contains or connects to those things, but they serve the operation rather than define it.

## How To Use It

1. **Plan**: build rosters, set coverage requirements, and manage leave and planned absence
2. **Reality**: track attendance, handle absence and no-shows, and find cover when the day changes
3. **Pay**: reconcile worked hours into payroll-ready outputs with an audit trail
4. Use the **sidebar** to navigate, or click the **help icon (?)** on any page for guidance
5. **Contact support** if you need additional assistance

## Key Features

### For Staff
- **My Leave**: Check your leave balance and request time off
- **My Schedule**: See your upcoming shifts and room assignments
- **Wallchart**: View the team calendar

### For Managers
- **Dashboard**: Your daily command center
- **Roster**: Build and publish schedules
- **Employees**: Manage team profiles
- **Leave Requests**: Approve or deny time-off requests
- **Payroll**: Export data for payroll processing

## Tips

- Start with the Dashboard each day
- Check the Wallchart before approving leave
- Build rosters at least a week in advance
