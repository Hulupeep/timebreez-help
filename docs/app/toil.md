# Time in Lieu (TOIL) Help

{: .note }
This page has not been checked against the current app yet. The pages listed under [Page by page]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/app/) as checked are up to date.


## What It Does

Track Time Off In Lieu (TOIL) - when staff work extra hours and earn compensatory time off.

## Why It Matters

Keep overtime fair and transparent. Staff can see what they've earned and take it when convenient.

## How To Use It

1. TOIL is recorded when **staff work beyond** their scheduled hours
2. The balance appears in their **leave section**
3. Staff can **request TOIL leave** like any other leave type

## Common Questions

### How is TOIL calculated?

Based on extra hours worked beyond scheduled shifts, typically hour-for-hour or as configured by your organization.

## Tips

- Review TOIL balances regularly
- Encourage staff to use accrued TOIL
