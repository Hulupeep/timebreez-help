# Control Desk Help

## What It Does

Real-time operations view showing who's in, who's out, and current room coverage.

## Why It Matters

Get an instant snapshot of your operations right now. Perfect for managing day-to-day surprises and last-minute changes.

## How To Use It

1. View the **live status** of all rooms
2. See **real-time clock-in data** (if Paxton integrated)
3. **Spot coverage gaps** immediately
4. Use **quick actions** to find cover or move staff

## Tips

- Keep Control Desk open during busy periods
- Use it to respond quickly to call-ins
