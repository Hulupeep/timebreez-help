# Billing Help

## What It Does

Manage your Timebreez subscription, view invoices, and update payment methods.

## Why It Matters

Keep your subscription active to maintain access to all features and data.

## How To Use It

1. View your **current plan**
2. See **billing history** and download invoices
3. **Update payment methods** if needed
4. **Upgrade or change** plans

## Tips

- Keep payment details up to date
- Download invoices for expense reports
