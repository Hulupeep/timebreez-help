# Staff App

**Who uses it:** staff, on their phone. Address: `/staff`. The bar at the bottom has **Home**, **Schedule**, **Leave**, **Team** and **Settings**.

Press the **?** at the top of the screen to open help for the page you are on.

## Home (Today)

Shows your shift today, or "No shift scheduled today", your leave balance, and quick actions: **View Schedule**, **Book time off** and **View Team**. **Book time off** (under your balance, or in the quick actions) opens the leave form straight away.

## Schedule

Your shifts for the week. Use the arrows to go to the previous or next week, or press **This Week**.

## Leave

Your leave balance, a form to request time off, and your requests.

1. Press **Book time off**.
2. Pick the **Start Date** and **End Date**, then the **Leave Type**.
3. For one day only, you can tick **Half day** and choose **Morning** or **Afternoon**. It is charged as half a day, and **My Requests** shows, for example, "Half day (morning)".
4. Add a note if you like, and press **Submit Request**. You see "Request submitted for approval".

If you are already off on those dates, the form says nothing comes out of your balance and tells you what to do: to book the days as another type, cancel the leave you have first, then book again.

If only some of the dates clash with leave you already have, the request is not sent. The message names the days that are still free, so you can change the dates to leave out the clash.

**Public Holiday** is not in your list of leave types. Public holidays come from your organisation's holiday calendar, so you don't book them.

If you don't have enough leave left, the message says how much the booking needs, your balance and how much you are short, in days with the hours, for example "Not enough leave: this needs 3 days (24h) and the balance is 2 days (16h), so it is 1 day (8h) short."

Booking in a leave year that isn't set up yet is refused with "The leave year that … falls in is not open yet". Ask an admin to open it.

A leave type that comes out of another type's balance (for example a type set to come out of Annual Leave) is checked against that balance, so you see the Annual Leave you have left. Requests of that type still waiting for a decision count in **awaiting approval** on the Annual Leave card. See [Leave Types]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/app/leave-types/).

There is one balance card for each allowance you have, for example Annual Leave. It shows:

- what you have left, for example **18 days left**, and **of 22 days for 2026**;
- what is **awaiting approval**, or **Nothing awaiting approval**;
- when something is waiting, what you would have left if it is approved, for example **16 days left if approved**. If a waiting request has no figure yet, the card says how much it takes off is not worked out yet, and shows no "if approved" figure;
- a line saying how it is worked out: leave already approved has been taken off, and the days are at your working day, for example "Days are at your 8h working day, so 18 days is 144h". If your working day is not confirmed yet, the card shows hours and says so.

Every figure comes from Timebreez itself, the same as your manager sees.

Leave types that don't come out of an allowance (for example Sick Leave or Unpaid Leave) get no card. They are listed once under **Other leave you can book**. That list also names types that come out of another allowance, and any allowance you should have but that has nothing on file yet: ask your manager about those.

Once next year is open, a line under the card shows next year's balance, for example "2027: 20 days left (carry-over added at year end)". Leave you book in next year comes off that, not this year's balance. You can cancel a pending request with **Cancel Request**, and its hours come off "awaiting approval" on the card straight away. Cancelling leave that is already approved puts the time back on your balance: "Approved leave cancelled — balance credited back".

Prefer your phone's chat app? [Link Telegram]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/app/my-settings/) and send `/off` with a date.

## Team

Who is on each day, room by room. Use the arrows or pick a date.

## Settings

**Appearance** comes first: **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (follows your phone's setting). It is saved on this phone only. Then **Connect Telegram**, so you can link the bot from your phone (see [Link Telegram]({{ site.baseurl }}/docs/app/my-settings/#link-telegram)). Under it are your own notification choices.
